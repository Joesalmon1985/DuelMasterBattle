## Joe's playtest checklist

| Check | Expected behaviour |
|---|---|
| Enter the battle | One faction owns the settlement and its active factories. Opposing faction has invading soldiers. Unit shapes identify types; colours and labels identify factions. |
| Move immediately | Walk away from spawn in every direction without getting trapped. Controls remain responsive while units fight. |
| Click a distant unit | A description appears, labelled something like `Red Skirmisher`. Clicking it does not move the wizard or cast anything. |
| Approach and click again | An attached choice card appears with Observe / Buff… / Destroy, plus a way to close it. The permanent spell toolbar is gone. |
| Open the choices | Combat and Game Time pause while you choose. Closing resumes them without jumping forward. World Turn does not increase. |
| Choose a buff | You can choose Shield, Attack speed or Range. The selected unit shows a clear effect or confirmation identifying the buff. |
| Choose Destroy | That individual unit dies. Other units remain unaffected. The destruction survives saving and loading. |
| Cancel or walk away | The card closes, nothing is cast, and movement works normally. |
| Enter the hazard test | Three labelled manifestations appear at three different boundary approaches. They remain visible while you walk and wait. |
| Click a hazard | From a distance you get an observation. Nearby, an eligible hazard offers Challenge. |
| Accept Challenge | The actual deduction duel opens, with colour guesses and match feedback. There is no three-click Channel shortcut. |
| Finish the duel | Winning removes the selected cube only. Losing does not cause Game Over. The world resumes correctly, and treatment allowances persist across Wait/save/load. |
| Resize and reload | Portrait and landscape keep choices and controls accessible. Reload preserves your valid position and the world changes you made. |

## Decisive criterion

Do not accept G04 merely because automated checks pass. The decisive test is whether you can walk up to a moving soldier, click it, choose a spell naturally, and see the correct persistent result—and then do the equivalent observation/challenge flow with a visible hazard.

**Experience question:** Do intervention and competing threats feel powerful and readable?
