## Joe's playtest

1. Start inside the real home settlement (`bash tools/play_g05.sh`). Find the obvious blocked south path (large grey **Boulder**).
2. Observe / Inspect the Rockfall up close (worker hint). Talk to **any**
   village worker. Choose the option asking them to clear the rocks (not the
   occupation / never-mind options).
3. Watch **that** worker walk to the rockfall and the stones slide aside. Walk
   through the newly opened exit (one World Turn).
4. Return, talk to the same worker (completion acknowledgement only after clear).
   Save/reload and leave/return — same IDs, no duplicates.
5. Optionally explore other nodes / a second settlement to confirm the full board still feels like one world.

## Expected observations

- The whole board exists as one world; every node is the same LocalArea size.
- The Rockfall is a real persistent world object (stone cluster, not a label-only prop).
- Rejected Travel while blocked says **The rockfall blocks the way.** and spends no World Turn.
- Helper Person is chosen by dialogue; occupation stays unchanged (activity temporarily “Clearing rockfall”).
- After completion the stones stay beside the path.
- No Mara shortage dialogue, demon cube or sluice entrance in this scenario.

**Experience question:** Does this feel like a persistent world where talking to a person can cause a simple, understandable change in that world?
