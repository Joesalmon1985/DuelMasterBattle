# Narrator voice — Duel Master Battle

One page. Applies to every `narrate()` / `say("", …)` string and every readable
(sign, logs, corpse, pickup text). NPC speech has its own voices and is exempt.

## Stance
- **Second person, present tense.** "You take the staff." Never "John takes".
  The protagonist changes (Halvard → John); the pronoun does not.
- **Dry and omniscient.** The narrator knows more than you and says slightly
  less than it knows. It never explains a joke.
- **Self-correcting.** When the story pivots, the narrator owns it in one line
  ("This is not, it turns out, his story.") and moves on. No apologies, no
  fourth-wall winking beyond that single beat.

## Register
- Short declaratives. One image per sentence. Cut adjectives that don't earn
  their place.
- Mechanics in parentheses and capitals, once, at the moment they matter:
  `(WOUNDED: −1 cast in your next duel.)`. Not restated later.
- Humour is allowed in ordinary beats (chests, villagers, the Fly). It is
  **not** allowed on: deaths, the Halvard pivot, Trial entry, the left-for-dead
  wake, injury reveals. Those land flat and plain.

## Never
- Copy or lightly paraphrase prose from any published game or book. The
  Deathtrap Dungeon *structure* is canon; its sentences are not ours to use.
- Third-person John as narrative subject outside NPC speech.
- Stale-state phrases: "try again", "the run resets", "the Blue Wizard passed
  through", "back to the gate".
- Signs that only say "dead end". A branch either goes somewhere or shows why
  it doesn't (rockfall, water, a body).

## Clues
Every hazard tells you what it will do before it does it — scratches on a
floor, a groove in the stone, a warning scraped into an idol's base. The
player may still choose wrong; the narrator does not gloat when they do.
