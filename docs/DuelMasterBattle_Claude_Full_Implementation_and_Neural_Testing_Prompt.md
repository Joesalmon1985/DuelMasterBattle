## E. Neural-network training and adversarial testing across ALL systems

I specifically want **real neural-network-driven gameplay testing**, not just scripted bots renamed as AI. Implement a reusable training/evaluation harness over the *actual* headless game adapters. - **RTS learning:** imitation learning from the scripted AI, then masked policy-gradient/PPO or another justified algorithm; reward actual productive economy, construction, unit production, defended settlements, military objectives, tech progression and survival. Penalise illegal actions, idle exploit loops and losses appropriately. Run longer episodes across all tribes; assess learned policy against scripted and no-op baselines on held-out maps/seeds.
- **Quests/dungeons:** curriculum over simple to multi-stage and branching tasks, navigation, evidence gathering, puzzle solving and recovery after world changes. Evaluate completion rate, legitimate alternate endings, travel/action efficiency, soft locks and unique failure paths. - **Duels:** train policies for player casting and opponents, compare to exact deduction/search solvers and random baseline, evaluate under differing rulesets, ensure no leakage of hidden codes. Save reproducible counterexample traces for illegal feedback or unwinnable bugs.
- **Outbreaks:** agents choose treatment, timing and location under limited attention while the economy/war AI continues. Evaluate containment rates, catastrophes averted, avoidable losses, and whether balancing makes unavoidable death common or trivialises the challenge.
- **Multi-system agents:** build a higher-level wizard agent whose actions cross travel → dialogue → dungeon → duel → treatment → observation; learn/test strategic prioritisation between competing settlement crises. Do not pretend an RTS-trained policy can do dialogue or puzzles without its own action semantics.

Compare policy performance with baselines rather than declaring success because training reward rose. Preserve deterministic execution when the policy, seed and sampled actions are fixed.
- Training is **an additional robustness/discovery tool**, not a substitute for hard-coded invariants, property-based tests, exhaustive small puzzle checks, numerical parity or headed screenshot tests. Keep the quick regression suite CPU-cheap; make long neural training/nightly stress optional and reproducible.
