# DuelMasterBattle — repository entry

The active development is the wizard-led hybrid in `WizardRTSHybrid/`. Start at `WizardRTSHybrid/CLAUDE.md` and `WizardRTSHybrid/AGENTS.md`. The approved direction is `docs/DuelMasterBattle_Design_v0_5.md`; the decision register is `docs/DECISION_REGISTER.md`.

The rest of this repository (`sim/dmb`, `godot_project`, `tests`, `DD`, `python_prototype`, older `docs/*`) is the earlier Catan-era game and its quest, dialogue, dungeon and Mastermind duel assets. Reuse them, do not delete them, and treat their economy/map rules as superseded (see `docs/CURRENT_STATUS_AND_GAPS.md` section 5). Root `README.md` is a historical Village Test note.

Approved programme (owner authorised an autonomous implementation run on 12 Oct 2026): `docs/PLAN_FULL_PROGRAMME_V2.md` (supersedes parts of `docs/PLAN_ECONOMY_TECH_ERA_PROGRAMME.md`). Its live state is `docs/ledger/LEDGER.md` (+ `RUNBOOK.md`, `QUESTIONS.md`) and the run report `docs/V2_RUN_REPORT.md`; the v2 and v3 work has since been merged into `main` (PRs #14, #15, #17).

**Current programme (approved by the owner 14 Oct 2026, decisions D33/D34):** `docs/PLAN_HIERARCHICAL_NEUROEVOLUTION.md` (the design) and `docs/EXECUTION_PLAN_NEUROEVOLUTION.md` (the step-by-step implementation guide; progress in `docs/ledger/NEUROEVO_PROGRESS.md`). The imported content roster is **frozen permanently**: no new buildings, units, wares, items or liquids from Widelands, OpenRA, Mindustry or any other game (plan v2's content waves P6/P7/P9/P11 are superseded); fixing and rebalancing existing entities continues. The development priority is autonomous faction play by hierarchical neuroevolution, trained with `train_ai.py` (root launchers `Train AI.bat` and friends). Restart point for the implementer: `docs/NEXT_AGENT_HANDOFF.md`, first section.

**Integrated on `main` (15 Oct 2026):** the three workstreams were merged together, see the first section of `docs/NEXT_AGENT_HANDOFF.md`. Besides the neuroevolution programme, `main` carries the wizard campaign, narrative, dialogue and quests (`docs/WIZARD_IMPLEMENTATION_HANDOFF.md`, ledger `docs/ledger/WIZARD_STORIES_PROGRESS.md`) and LAN multiplayer with PvP Ward duels and Android packaging (`docs/MOBILE_LAN_MULTIPLAYER.md`).
